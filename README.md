# Global Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 1,000 global sales records (2010-2017) across 185 countries, 7 regions and 12 product types.

![Dashboard](PowerBI%20Sales%20Dashboard.png)

## Key Insights
- Total profit is about 391M on about 1.33B in revenue, with 5.05M units sold.
- Europe and Sub-Saharan Africa drive about 53% of total profit.
- The offline channel contributes about 53% of profit (207.89M), versus 183.32M online.
- Cosmetics is the top profit category.
- Revenue peaked in 2014. The 2017 drop is because the data ends in July 2017.

## What the Dashboard Shows
- Total profit by sales channel
- Revenue and profit by region
- Revenue by country and by order year
- Units sold by region and by item type
